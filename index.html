05 / UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

What remains in orbit.

Space debris is a sustainability challenge that grows with activity in Earth's orbital environment.

01
✧

What is space debris?

Human-made objects in orbit that no longer serve a useful purpose. Examples include retired spacecraft, spent rocket stages and fragments created by break-ups or collisions.

02
↗

Why is it increasing?

More launches and satellite deployments add objects to orbit. Explosions, collisions and other fragmentation events can create many additional pieces.

03
⌁

Why does it matter?

Objects travel at high orbital speeds. Even small fragments can damage spacecraft, while operators must monitor and manage close approaches.

04
∞

Kessler Syndrome

A scenario in which collisions generate more debris, increasing the likelihood of further collisions and potentially making some orbital regions harder to use.

THE WAY FORWARD

Design for a sustainable orbit.

Responsible mission design, passivation, post-mission disposal, improved tracking, coordination and active debris-removal research all contribute to long-term orbital sustainability.
